01 — about

Who I am

I'm Areum Seo — a localization professional, linguist, and builder working across Korean, Japanese, and English. I came to Tokyo on a MEXT scholarship, earned a master's degree in applied linguistics at Tokyo University of Foreign Studies, and taught Japanese at Waseda before moving into industry. Teaching is where I learned to explain a system to someone who has never seen it — which turns out to be most of what I still do.

Since then, my work has spanned localization QA, translation, project management, and language operations — including Alexa AI data operations at Amazon Japan, where I received a Customer Obsession Award, and nearly four years as an in-house interpreter and translator at Kakao Piccoma, facilitating Korean, Japanese, and English across international operations, business development, and analysis. At Snowflake, I build production AI systems: LLM evaluation pipelines, RAG services, agentic workflows, and governed data intelligence. Most recently, I demonstrated CoWork to visitors at the Snowflake World Tour Seoul experience zone: same instinct as the classroom, shorter attention span.

I build. Responsive web apps and a Discord agent — using TypeScript, Python, FastAPI, PostgreSQL, and Claude Code. I hold a SnowPro Associate certification and credentials in data engineering and DevOps on AWS. Localization taught me to treat language as a system; building is how I get to change one.

Find Your Sole

A personalized shoe recommendation service for running shoes and everyday comfort shoes. Answer a few questions about your feet, lifestyle, and budget — get matched with the right shoe, explained by AI. Now a responsive web app that works the same on desktop and mobile, with Korean and English throughout. Built mainly with Claude Code, with Codex as a second pair of eyes.

ProblemChoosing a shoe means weighing arch, pronation, terrain, mileage and budget all at once.
IterationStarted in May as a Streamlit prototype, then a FastAPI backend and a Flutter app, and now this web app. In October it widened from running shoes to everyday comfort shoes. An in-app feedback inbox, a note plus an optional screenshot, collects what people actually run into.

02

Stockpulse Select

A dashboard for discovering, comparing and keeping an eye on Korean and US stocks and ETFs. Claude proposes candidates and writes the notes, but every price and return is verified against Naver Finance data, so invented tickers never reach the screen. Compare up to four holdings on normalized performance, volatility and drawdown, with a what-if simulation drawn from three years of daily closes. Runs in the browser on desktop and mobile, in Korean and English — sign-in is invite-only while it’s a prototype. Built the same way: mainly Claude Code, with some Codex.

ProblemAn AI that finds stocks can just as easily invent a ticker or a return.
Evaluation & guardrailsEvery price and return is verified against Naver Finance data — hallucinated tickers never reach the screen. AI-generated notes containing figures or hype language are held back; funds without sufficient history show “—” rather than a fabricated return.
IterationFive tagged releases between Sept 28 and Oct 5 (v0.1.2 to v0.1.7). New ETF listings require a confirmed listing date before appearing in results.
Flutter Python FastAPI PostgreSQL Claude Render Web

Moon Rabbit’s Way Home

A Chuseok runner game in the spirit of the Chrome dino. Help the moon rabbit get home for the holiday by jumping over relatives’ nagging — “Married yet?”, “How much do you make?” — and grabbing songpyeon along the way. Built in a day with Claude Code as a single HTML file, with Korean and English versions localized rather than translated so every nag lands in both languages, plus shareable score cards.

IterationBuilt in a day, then fixed what real phones exposed: with no viewport tag they showed the desktop layout, and the game-over panel didn’t fit a 120px-tall canvas, so it now opens below the canvas on small screens.
